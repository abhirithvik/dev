🛠️ Visa Application Process

1. Initial Consultation

Personal evaluation of your background, goals, and eligibility.

Discussion of suitable visa options based on your profile.

Clarification of timelines, costs, and documentation needed.

Understanding of country-specific immigration procedures.

Addressing your queries and setting realistic expectations.

2. Document Review

We create a tailored checklist based on your chosen visa category.

Each document is reviewed for completeness and accuracy.

We assist in gathering financial, academic, or employment records.

Translation or notarization support is provided if required.

All paperwork is aligned with embassy or consulate requirements.

3. SOP / Cover Letter

We help draft a compelling Statement of Purpose or Cover Letter.

The content is customized to match the visa officer’s expectations.

We highlight your motivations, plans, and supporting evidence clearly.

Our editors ensure the language is professional and persuasive.

Final drafts are proofread and formatted for official submission.

4. Online Application Submission

We fill and submit your application accurately through official portals.

Applications are submitted on VFS, IRCC, or relevant embassy platforms.

Each form is double-checked to ensure no mistakes or missing info.

We assist in uploading all necessary documents as per embassy format.

Ongoing tracking and updates are provided until a final decision.

5. Fee Payment & Biometrics

We guide you through the visa fee payment process securely.

Biometric appointments are scheduled as per your availability.

We ensure all receipts and confirmations are submitted properly.

Guidance is offered for uploading financial and accommodation proofs.

We help resolve issues like payment failures or portal errors.

6. Interview Preparation

Mock interview sessions are conducted for high-confidence delivery.

We cover common and profile-specific questions.

Real-time feedback is provided on tone, content, and clarity.

Special training for US, UK, Germany, and Canada interviews.

We help reduce anxiety and boost visa interview success rate.

7. Post-Visa Guidance

Support with booking flights and choosing the best route options.

We assist with blocked accounts, forex cards, and health insurance.

Help with arrival planning and accommodation search abroad.

Orientation on local laws, travel tips, and settlement steps.

We stay connected even after visa approval for smooth relocation.

📅 Typical Visa Timelines

  • USA: 2–6 weeks – F1, H1B, and B1/B2 visas vary by season and embassy availability.
  • Canada: 4–8 weeks – Applies to study permits, Express Entry, and visitor visas. Biometrics may extend timelines.
  • UK: ~15 working days – Standard processing for Tier 4 student and skilled worker visas. Priority services available.
  • Germany: 3–6 weeks – For student and job seeker visas. Appointment wait time can vary significantly.
  • Australia: 4–10 weeks – Covers student (subclass 500) and work visas. Includes character and health checks.
  • New Zealand: 4–6 weeks – Common for study and post-study work visas. Online submission preferred.
  • France: 2–4 weeks – For VLS-TS student and Schengen visas. Quick if documents are in order.
  • Italy: 3–5 weeks – National visa for students and dependents. Requires full documentation upfront.
  • Ireland: 3–6 weeks – Study and job offer-based visas. Postal application possible for some types.
  • Sweden: 2–8 weeks – For university admissions, especially autumn intake. Online application speeds up process.
