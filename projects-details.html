Our Software Projects

Explore Our Latest Projects

We have successfully delivered over 270+ customized software projects for PhD research, startups, and enterprises. Here are five of our recent projects:

1. PhD Research Publication Tool

Overview: A web-based tool designed for PhD researchers to organize and manage their research papers, citations, and sources. It also offers features for citation generation, paper formatting, and plagiarism checking.

Technologies: React.js, Node.js, MongoDB, OpenAI API for plagiarism check

Impact: This tool has helped 500+ scholars streamline their research and publication process, making it more efficient and organized.

2. Smart Campus Management System

Overview: A comprehensive system for managing college and university campuses, including student data, faculty schedules, event management, and communication tools for students and staff.

Technologies: Angular, PHP, MySQL, Firebase

Impact: This system has been deployed in over 10 campuses, improving administrative efficiency and enhancing student engagement.

3. AI-powered Job Recommendation Platform

Overview: A platform that matches job seekers with job openings based on their skills, experience, and preferences using machine learning algorithms.

Technologies: Python (TensorFlow, scikit-learn), Django, PostgreSQL

Impact: More than 50,000 job seekers have found suitable roles using this platform, helping bridge the gap between employers and talent.

4. E-commerce Web Application

Overview: A scalable e-commerce platform for small businesses, providing features like product catalog management, customer management, and online payments.

Technologies: Vue.js, Laravel, Stripe API, MySQL

Impact: Over 100 businesses have launched their online stores using this platform, increasing their sales by up to 40%.

5. Virtual Conference Platform

Overview: A platform built for hosting virtual conferences, with features like live streaming, session management, attendee networking, and virtual exhibitor booths.

Technologies: React.js, WebRTC, Node.js, MongoDB

Impact: This platform has hosted over 30 conferences with thousands of attendees, revolutionizing the virtual conference experience.
